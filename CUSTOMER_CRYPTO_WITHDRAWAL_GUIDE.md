# Customer Crypto Withdrawal Guide

## 🎯 How to Withdraw Your Crypto to Trust Wallet

This guide will help you withdraw your cryptocurrency from your POS account to your Trust Wallet or any other external wallet.

---

## ⚡ Quick Start (3 Steps)

### Step 1: Get Your Trust Wallet Address
1. Open **Trust Wallet** app on your phone
2. Find and tap on the crypto you want to receive (e.g., **USDT**)
3. Tap the **"Receive"** button
4. Your wallet address will appear - **Copy it**

### Step 2: Select Network That Matches Your Wallet
Make sure the network you select matches the address you copied:
- **Tron (TRC-20)**: If your USDT address starts with **"T..."**
- **BSC (BEP-20)**: If your address starts with **"0x..."** and you selected BSC network
- **Polygon**: If your address starts with **"0x..."** and you selected Polygon network

### Step 3: Complete Withdrawal in POS System
1. Click **"Withdraw"** button next to your crypto balance
2. Select the **crypto asset** and **network**
3. Paste your **Trust Wallet address**
4. Enter the **amount** to withdraw
5. Double-check everything
6. Click **"Send"**

## ⚠️ CRITICAL: Network Must Match Address!

### ✅ CORRECT Examples:
| What You See | Network to Select | Why |
|--------------|-------------------|-----|
| Address starts with **T** | **tron** | Tron network address |
| Address starts with **0x** + BSC | **bsc** | Binance Smart Chain |
| Address starts with **0x** + Polygon | **polygon** | Polygon network |
| Address starts with **bc1** | **bitcoin** | Bitcoin native SegWit |

### ❌ WRONG (Will Lose Funds!)
| What NOT To Do | Why It's Wrong |
|----------------|----------------|
| Tron address (T...) + BSC network | Different blockchain - funds lost |
| BSC address (0x...) + Tron network | Incompatible - funds lost |
| Ethereum address + Bitcoin network | Completely different format - rejected |

**🚨 IMPORTANT**: If you select the wrong network, your crypto may be lost permanently!

## 💡 Understanding Network Options

When you select a network, you'll see indicators:

### ⚡ Direct Rail
- Means: Sent directly on the blockchain (no middleman)
- Speed: Instant (on-chain confirmation time only)
- Fees: Network gas fees only (paid by system hot wallet)
- Privacy: High (no KYC, no exchange)
- **Networks**: Tron, BSC, Polygon for USDT

### 🏦 Exchange-Mediated
- Means: Routed through exchange (KuCoin, Binance, etc.)
- Speed: May take minutes to hours
- Fees: Exchange withdrawal fees
- Privacy: Lower (exchange records)
- **Networks**: Ethereum (for some coins), Solana, etc.

**Pro Tip**: Always choose "Direct Rail" when available for faster, cheaper withdrawals!

## 🕐 How Long Does It Take?

### Direct Rail Withdrawals (Tron, BSC, Polygon):
1. **Deduction**: Instant (your balance updates immediately)
2. **Blockchain broadcast**: 1-3 seconds
3. **Network confirmation**: 3-30 seconds
4. **Appears in Trust Wallet**: Usually within 1 minute

### Exchange-Mediated Withdrawals:
1. **Deduction**: Instant
2. **Exchange processing**: 5-30 minutes
3. **Blockchain broadcast**: Varies
4. **Appears in Trust Wallet**: 10 minutes - 2 hours typically

**Note**: During high network congestion, all times may be longer.

## 🚨 Troubleshooting

### Problem: "Insufficient balance"
**Solution**: 
- Check your available balance (shown at top of withdrawal form)
- System may reserve small amount for fees
- Try reducing withdrawal amount slightly

### Problem: "Invalid address format"
**Solution**:
- Make sure address matches the network:
  - Tron addresses start with "T"
  - EVM addresses (BSC, Polygon, Ethereum) start with "0x"
  - Bitcoin addresses start with "bc1", "1", or "3"
- No spaces or extra characters
- Copy directly from Trust Wallet

### Problem: "Withdrawal pending" for long time
**Solution**:
- Check blockchain explorer:
  - Tron: https://tronscan.org
  - BSC: https://bscscan.com
  - Ethereum: https://etherscan.io
- Search your destination address
- Contact support if no transaction appears after 1 hour

### Problem: Sent to wrong address
**Solution**:
- Unfortunately, blockchain transactions are **irreversible**
- If you sent to wrong address on same network, funds are lost
- If you sent to wrong network, funds may be lost permanently
- Always double-check before sending!

### Problem: Not showing in Trust Wallet
**Solution**:
1. Wait 5 minutes and pull down to refresh
2. Check you're looking at the correct network:
   - For USDT: Make sure you're viewing the right network (Tron, BSC, etc.)
   - Trust Wallet shows each network separately
3. Update Trust Wallet to latest version
4. Check blockchain explorer - if transaction succeeded there, it will appear
