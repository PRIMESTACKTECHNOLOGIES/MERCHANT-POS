③ PAY OUT · Bank Withdraw
(Wallet → Your Bank Account)
Standard Bank Payout (T+0 to T+2)
1 Go to Wallet → Withdraw
2 Select Bank Transfer BANK
3 Pick or add your bank account (one-time save)
4 Enter the amount you want to withdraw
5 Review fees and net amount
6 Confirm with your PIN / Face ID / OTP
7 ✅ Sent for processing. Arrives in your bank:
• Wise ACH / Local → same day (T+0)
• SWIFT international → 1–2 business days
• MYR / AED local → same-day
Card Payout to Debit (Instant)
1 Go to Wallet → Withdraw → Card CARD
2 Select your saved Visa/Mastercard debit
3 Enter amount → confirm
4 ✅ Funds on your card in ~30 minutes
Required info (add once)
ℹ Full bank name, account number, account holder name
ℹ SWIFT / Routing or IBAN (international)

💡 Quick Reference — Pay In (Funding)

  • Card / Apple Pay → Instant. Works with any Visa/Mastercard in 180+ countries.
  • Local Bank → MYR, AED, USD, PKR, CDF — free, 0% fee for same-bank customers.
  • USDT Deposit (TRC20) → Fastest global option, arrives in ~1 minute, zero exchange hassle.
  • All methods credit the same wallet — mix and match as you like.

💡 Quick Reference — Payout Out (Withdrawal)

  • Bank via Wise → Cheapest international option (0.41% typical). Supported 150+ countries.
  • Instant Card Payout → Funds back on your own Visa debit card in under 30 minutes.
  • USDT TRC20 → Payout anywhere in the world in under 3 minutes. Minimal gas ($0.01 USD).
  • Security → All withdrawals require 2-factor authentication + email/SMS OTP.
